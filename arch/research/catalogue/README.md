# The complete idea catalogue: every idea from every research round (27–28 October 2026)

This is the long companion to [RESEARCH-SUMMARY.md](../RESEARCH-SUMMARY.md). It lists every idea, design option, blocker and
finding from all 54 reports in the nine rounds:
- main bets;
- ranked candidates;
- runners-up;
- every item from the reports' rejected lists.

Four reading agents extracted the entries from the full reports. Nothing was added that the reports don't say.

**Size:** 1109 catalogued entries, plus 362 one-line rejected items.

**Verdicts** (counting entries that have a verdict line):

| Verdict | Entries |
|---|---:|
| Kept / ONE bet | 267 |
| Runner-up | 110 |
| Narrowed | 99 |
| Killed / rejected | 230 |
| Other (findings, designs, measurements, mixed) | 399 |

**How to read an entry.** Each one has these fields, in order:
1. *What*
2. *How it would work*
3. *Evidence / numbers*, labelled **F** (sourced), **M** (measured), **I** (inference) or **E** (estimate)
4. *Prior art*
5. *Verdict*, with the report's scores where it gave any
6. *Kill test / first proof*

**Caveats:**
- Round numbering follows RESEARCH-SUMMARY.md. `moonshots2/00-critic` calls the `moonshots/` round "round 1"; here that round is **Round 2**.
- `flip/06-giants` and `flip2/04-codemode` give no explicit go / no-go for their bet. Their verdicts here are inferred from their kill conditions.
- Rounds 1 and 8 analyse designs and layers rather than listing ideas. Each of their findings, design options and blockers gets its own entry, so those rounds have many more entries.
- The figures are as the research agents reported them. Only the items marked "verified" in RESEARCH-SUMMARY.md §15 were re-checked against primary sources.
