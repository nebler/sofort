**Labels:** **F** = sourced metadata, title, description, program or chapter information; **I** = acquisition judgment. Ranks and predicted seed yield are **I**, not claims that a particular sentence was heard. No practitioner quotations are asserted. Long talks, Q&A and roadmaps receive priority; short EDA talks remain where they identify unusually specific capability gaps.

**Captions:** `A: en-orig` = English original automatic-caption track exposed by yt-dlp; `S: en` = English entry in its `subtitles` map. All 40 have an English track; this checks availability, not transcription accuracy. Dates are **YouTube upload dates**, not conference dates. In particular, NetEdit is a SIGCOMM 2024 talk uploaded in 2025.

**Verification method:** Used `~/.local/bin/yt-dlp` (2026.10.26), `ytsearch20:`, `--flat-playlist`, `--skip-download` and `--print '%()j'` for discovery. Selected videos then received full metadata extraction with `--skip-download --ignore-no-formats-error --print '%()j'`; retained ID, title, channel, duration, upload date, and the `subtitles`/`automatic_captions` language keys. Caption bodies were not requested; separate `--list-subs` calls were unnecessary. Serialized batch loops slept 10 seconds before each yt-dlp invocation; no HTTP 429 was observed. Full extraction durations take precedence over occasionally one-second-longer flat-search values. One display title is explicitly shortened below.
