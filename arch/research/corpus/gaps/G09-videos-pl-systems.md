## Verification and reading conventions

- **F:** Metadata was checked with `~/.local/bin/yt-dlp` version `2026.10.26`. Discovery used `--flat-playlist`, `ytsearch20:`, and `--print`; every selected ID then received a full per-video metadata extraction with `--skip-download --no-playlist --print '%()j'`.
- **F:** Consecutive yt-dlp calls were separated by at least 10 seconds, including sleeps in the serial verification loop. No HTTP 429 occurred. No media, caption files, or transcripts were downloaded; no `--list-subs` calls were needed.
- **F:** `yes` means an uploaded English subtitle track was advertised in `subtitles`; `auto` means an English track was advertised in `automatic_captions`. The checks inspected English language keys such as `en` and `en-orig`. This verifies track availability; caption text and accuracy remain for the lead's fetch pass.
- **F:** Dates below are **YouTube upload dates**, not event dates. For example, the P99 CONF 2025 recordings in this batch were uploaded in March 2026. Durations come from full video extraction; flat-playlist values sometimes differed by one second.
- **I:** The four 2024 selections are deliberate exceptions to the preference for newer material: the [CakeML keynote](https://pldi24.sigplan.org/details/pldi-2024-papers/100/Much-Still-to-Do-in-Compiler-Verification-A-Perspective-from-the-CakeML-Project-) explicitly identifies unfinished work, and the [SYCL 2024 program](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL5AY2Vv6EsfSgmueLbzGVJpWIw8cIFl8J) supplies concrete compiler, linking, and data-layout constraints.
