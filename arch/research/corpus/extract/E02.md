### Disagreements and unresolved predictions worth carrying into phase two

- **Q/I — Scale of realized progress:** Scott predicts repeated 10× work expansion and reports 91% automated initiation (10, 13:00–14:00). These use different denominators and samples; they are a tension, not a demonstrated numerical contradiction.
- **Q/I — Substitutability:** Scott says most model swaps go unnoticed (10, 12:00); Ree says local classifier options fall well short of Jev on his tests (11, 15:00–16:00); Dax says the same model can fail when served badly (13, 48:00–50:00). Task-specific adequacy and serving quality separate these claims.
- **Q/I — Prices:** Michelle expects less subsidized, more metered inference and tighter subscriptions within six to twelve months (12, 40:00–43:00). Dax offers both near-term scarcity and eventual overcapacity (13, 35:00–36:00/54:00–55:00); he explicitly disclaims predicting that prices will double. The horizons differ.
- **Q/I — Locality/scale:** Cloudflare's original smaller-model, geographically dispersed thesis was partly overturned by large models and throughput hardware (12, 18:00–19:00), while Sunil wants phone-sized classification (11, 16:00). This may describe distinct workload markets rather than one universal architecture winning.
- **Q/I — Human understanding:** Scott's outcome-owning virtual employee framing (10, 10:00) sits beside Ree's failed uncomprehended rewrites (11, 01:00:00/01:07:00), Demetri's control surfaces (09, 13:00–15:00) and Dax's unchanged architectural problems (13, 01:02:00). None establishes that a better future model cannot close the gap.
- **Q/I — Agent volume versus bottlenecks:** Scott's self-driving software (10, 13:00) increases initiation; Demetri's judgment interrupts (09, 54:00–01:00:00) and Dax's overwhelmed maintainers (13, 22:00) constrain useful throughput. Initiation and completion should not be conflated.
